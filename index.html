The name. The movement. The meaning.

So, what's a hippie?

Hippies were part of a cultural movement that began in the 1960s, especially in the US and UK. They questioned the rules, celebrated freedom, and believed there was more than one way to live.

Why we chose it

We're inspired by that independent spirit, not any one style or way of life. Hippies believed there was more than one way to live. We believe there's more than one way to build a business. Whatever yours looks like, you deserve a website that feels like you.

Freedom

Hippies valued personal choice and living life on their own terms.

For your websiteYou own your domain, your site and your accounts. No lock-in, ever.

Peace

They opposed war and believed in harmony and kindness.

For your websiteA calm, friendly process. No jargon, no pressure, no stress.

Creativity

Art, music and new ideas were at the heart of everything they did.

For your websiteFresh, expressive design with real personality. No boring templates.

Individuality

They shared, supported each other and looked out for their neighbours.

They cared about fairness, the planet and people's wellbeing.

For your websiteMore people, from every background, getting their ideas online.

In today's world, "hippie" often describes someone positive, open-minded, creative and down to earth. That's exactly how we approach every website we build.

Questions

Good vibes, straight answers.

Do I need to be a hippie to work with you?

Not at all. The name is about the spirit: freedom, creativity and doing things your way. We build calm, professional sites just as happily as colourful ones.

Is it really live in 24 hours?

Yes, for 1-page and small business websites. The clock starts once we have your payment and everything in the brief. Bigger websites take 3–5 days and shop and log-in websites 1–3 weeks, because bookings, shops and log-ins need extra care.

I'm not in the UK. Can you still help?

Yes. We build for people in the UK, United States, Canada, Africa and anywhere else. You can pay in pounds, US dollars or Canadian dollars.

I don't have a logo or photos yet. Is that OK?

Totally fine. We can create a simple text logo and use quality stock photos, so you can launch now and swap them in later.

Can you update my site after launch?

Yes. Website care includes up to 1 hour of changes every month. Without it, we charge a fixed hourly rate for updates.
